About Me

The Journey from Data Science to Web Development

I started my career as a data scientist, building predictive models and extracting insights from complex datasets. After five years diving deep into statistical analysis and machine learning, I realized I wanted to bring those insights to life through user interfaces and real-time applications.

The transition to fullstack development felt natural—I already understood the data layer; now I was learning to communicate it effectively to end users. This unique background allows me to build data-driven applications that are both technically sound and user-friendly.

Sports Analytics: A Personal Passion

Beyond work, I'm obsessed with sports analytics. Whether it's basketball player performance metrics, soccer expected goals (xG) models, or cricket win probability calculations, I love exploring how data reveals hidden patterns in sports. This passion often finds its way into my side projects.
